1. What conclusion can you reach about the responses to this question?
My conclusion is that majority of students have a second monitor, weather that is another laptop or a tablet. 8 of the students who took the survey don't have a second monitor. I also noticed that all the students will keep a proactive mindset during the course, by joining class early and coming prepared with reviewed notes and proactive mindsets. As per the notetaking question, the class seems divided 50/50 with half taking notes with pen & paper and the other half in their computer (2 students will use both ways). 
For the last part of the survey, the data was not as easy to categorize. All students had different questions/concerns regarding this course. 2 people did ask about tutoring, and 4 people did not respond/didn't have questions.

2. Is the default summary or visualization helpful? How could it be improved?
The default visualizations for the open-ended questions are not helpful for identifying trends because answers are often long and wording between students is varied. This issue can be improved by selecting key phrases from responses such as "drinking coffee," "plugging in devices", and "reviewing notes" and creating a bar chart to manually plug in how often a response includes the phrase or similar phrasing to better identify which kind of response leads.
